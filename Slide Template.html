The loss landscape

  • A tiny neural net with two weights. The floor is every setting of the weights; height is its cross-entropy loss on the data.
  • Training is walking downhill. Same start, three optimizers, different paths.
training loss

drag the surface to turn it

0Drag the surface to turn it. Two weights, so the loss is a hill you can look at. Real models have billions of weights, but the picture is the same idea.

1Point at the flat parts: plateaus, where the gradient is tiny and training stalls. Point at the two valleys: two different networks that fit the data equally well.

2Press Train. Then switch optimizer with the same start and press Train again. Momentum keeps its speed through the flat part; Adam takes even steps whatever the slope. Push the learning rate up and watch SGD bounce.
